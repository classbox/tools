# 🌊 Tsunami Lab — Teacher's Guide (Grade 2)

Open **[`tsunami-lab.html`](tsunami-lab.html)** in Chrome or Edge on your classroom projector.

---

## 🔬 What Students See

1. **⚡ Fault Over Magma**: In the cutaway cross-section under the deep ocean, glowing magma heats the crust beneath a subduction fault. Triggering an earthquake snaps the seafloor upward!
2. **⬆️ Water Pushed Up**: Glowing upward energy arrows, shockwave rings, and rising bubbles show the seafloor shoving the whole column of ocean water straight up to the surface.
3. **📈 Slows Down & Piles Up**: As the wave enters shallow water on the continental slope, animated cutaway arrows and wave compression show the front slowing down while the back catches up—piling the water tall!
4. **🌊 Drawback & Breaker Crash**: Notice how the water pulls back off the sand first, then curls forward into a 3D plunging breaker that explodes with whitewater foam.
5. **🏘️ Procedural Village Surge & High Ground**: Watch water rush around buildings, snap pier pilings and stilt cabanas, toss moored boats, uproot articulated palm trees, and tumble cars—while the **Hilltop Lighthouse** stays safely above the flood!

---

## 🎮 Controls & Shortcuts

- **Quake Strength (`1`–`9`)**: Slide from tiny ripples (`1–2`) to coastal surf (`4–5`) up to a Mega Tsunami (`8–9`).
- **💥 Cause Earthquake!** (`Space` or `Q`): Triggers the offshore megathrust fault rupture.
- **🐢 Slow-Mo** (`S`): Slows time to `0.36x` so students can clearly watch the upward water push and curling wave lip.
- **⏸️ Pause** (`P`): Freeze the wave mid-curl or mid-surge to discuss with the class!
- **🎲 New Village** (`N`): Generates a fresh procedural beach & village layout.
- **🏗️ Rebuild** (`0`): Repairs the current village & resets the ocean so you can compare different earthquake strengths or shoreline defenses on the exact same town.
- **Shoreline Defense Modes** (left sidebar): Compare **🏖️ Open Beach**, **🧱 Seawall**, and **🌿 Mangroves**!
- **Camera Views (`1`–`6`)**:
  - `1` **Bird's-Eye**: Whole diorama from offshore fault to hillside.
  - `2` **Side Cutaway**: Geological cross-section showing magma, fault, upward push, and curling wave profile.
  - `3` **Fault & Magma**: Zoomed underwater cross-section right at the fault.
  - `4` **Beach POV**: Stand on the golden sand and watch the wave tower toward you!
  - `5` **Village POV**: Street-level view inside town as the flood surge rushes in.
  - `6` **Wave Chase**: Follows the tsunami crest all the way from the seafloor to Main Street!
  - Freely **drag to orbit**, **scroll to zoom** right into street level, **right-drag to pan**, or use **Arrow keys** to walk around.
- **Clean View (`H`)**: Hides all HUD panels for distraction-free projection.
