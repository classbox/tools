Grade 2 Science Guide
  • 1. Fault Over Magma: Hot magma heats the mantle beneath ocean plates. When stuck plates snap upward, the seafloor jumps up!
  • 2. Water Pushed Up: The rising seafloor shoves a whole column of ocean water straight up to the surface, creating a big dome.
  • 3. Slows & Piles Up: In deep water, the wave races fast and low. As the seafloor rises near the beach, the front slows down while the back catches up—so the wave piles tall!
  • 4. Drawback & Crash: Often the trough arrives first, pulling water off the beach before the giant curling breaker slams ashore.
  • 5. High Ground: Notice how the hilltop lighthouse always stays safe above the flood surge! Try the Seawall and Mangrove modes to compare protection.
