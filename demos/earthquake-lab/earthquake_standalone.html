Teacher notes (the real science)
  • Elastic rebound: plates move steadily, but the fault is locked by friction. Rock near the fault bends and stores energy (watch the road bend) until friction is overcome – then it snaps back and slips suddenly.
  • Slide Past (1 & 2): transform boundary – plates rub past each other horizontally.
  • Push Under (1 & 2): subduction – heavy ocean crust sinks under lighter land crust, dragging the coast down until it snaps back up (making a tsunami wave) and feeding a volcano.
  • Smash Together (1 & 2): continental collision – two thick land plates crash head-on, crumpling and lifting tall snow-capped mountains higher with each quake.
